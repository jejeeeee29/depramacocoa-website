SEED TO POWDER

Our Four-Step Production Pipeline

01

Selected Cocoa Beans

We source and select cocoa beans based on origin, quality, and preprocessing requirements to ensure reliable raw materials for industrial applications.

02

Controlled Roasting & Grinding

Cocoa beans are carefully roasted and ground under controlled processing conditions to develop a consistent cocoa mass profile.

03

Cocoa Mass & Butter Extraction

Cocoa mass is processed through hydraulic pressing to efficiently separate cocoa butter from cocoa cake, producing two essential cocoa ingredients

04

Cocoa Powder Milling & Quality Control

The cocoa cake is finely milled into cocoa powder with controlled fat content, color, alkalization, and particle size, followed by quality checks to ensure batch-to-batch consistency.
