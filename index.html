OUR PRODUCT RANGE

Engineered Cocoa Ingredients for Every Formulation

Natural Cocoa Powder

Pure, light brown powder expressing raw cocoa acidity and authentic fruity notes. Ideal for premium baking & confection.

Alkalized (Dutch-Process)

Dutch-processed to neutralize acidity. Highly soluble with deep warm brown tones, rich rounded chocolate flavor.

Deep Black Alkalized

Ultra-alkalized intense dark profile. Ideal for cookies, dark frosting and striking visual chocolate formulations.

Cocoa Butter

Smooth, ivory-colored cocoa butter used in chocolate making, cosmetics and confections. Rich creamy fat extracted from cocoa beans.

Cocoa Liquor

Also known as cocoa liquor, a thick dark paste made from ground cocoa nibs. The base ingredient for all chocolate products.
